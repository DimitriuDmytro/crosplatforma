## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the warmth, craft, and visceral appetite of culinary exploration within a modern, focused dark interface. Designed for food enthusiasts and everyday cooks, the experience evokes the comforting, intimate atmosphere of an evening kitchen—soft glowing heat, fresh ingredients, and tactile surfaces.

Drawing from Material 3 Expressive principles combined with plush, modern container styling, the interface balances high-contrast legibility with rich, comforting depth. Interfaces feel welcoming and generous rather than clinical, leading with lush food photography, friendly geometric typography, and gentle ember-toned highlights.

## Colors

The palette establishes an appetizing dark canvas built on espresso and cocoa undertones rather than cold grays, paired with high-vibrance culinary accents.

- **Primary (`#FF7A29`):** Radiant warm orange representing heat, citrus, and zest. Drives primary calls-to-action, key highlights, and active states.
- **Secondary (`#48A560`):** Fresh botanical sage/emerald green reflecting herbs, crisp produce, and dietary indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#E0A96D`):** Warm toasted crust and honey tone used for ratings, secondary tags, and subtle badges.
- **Neutral Surface Foundations:**
  - Background Canvas: `#14100E` (Deep espresso black)
  - Surface Base: `#1E1714` (Dark warm slate)
  - Surface Container: `#29201B` (Plush recipe cards and modules)
  - Surface Container High: `#362B25` (Elevated controls, active segments, bottom sheets)
  - Outlines / Dividers: `#3D3028` (Warm low-contrast structural borders)
- **Typography & Content:**
  - Text Primary: `#FBF3EB` (Warm cream white for optimal readability against dark surfaces)
  - Text Secondary: `#B3A296` (Muted warm beige for nutritional stats, metadata, and timestamps)

## Typography

Typography pairs friendly, open geometry with clean utilitarian legibility. 

- **Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Offers organic terminals and wide proportions, delivering an appetizing, welcoming presence for dish titles, editorial callouts, and category headers.
- **Body & Labels (Inter):** Ensures clear scanning of ingredient measurements, prep times, and cooking instructions in dynamic kitchen lighting.
- **Hierarchy:** Generous leading on body copy preserves reading ease across multi-step instructions, while tighter letter-spacing on display headlines yields cohesive titles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an 8-point base grid focused on comfortable hand reach and spacious visual breathing room.

- **Mobile Viewports (<600px):** Single-column presentation, 16px horizontal margins (`margin`), and 16px gutters (`gutter`). Recipe cards, horizontal carousel rows, and nutrition banners occupy full content width to maximize tap targets.
- **Tablet & Larger (>600px):** 8-column to 12-column layouts with 24px–32px gutters, enabling two-column recipe split screens (ingredients docked on the left, method instructions on the right).
- **Internal Spacing:** Standardized on `space-md` (16px) for inner card padding and element stacks to maintain an open, relaxed reading rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established primarily through Material 3 tonal layering over pure black drop shadows, maintaining warm ambiance and avoiding muddy surfaces.

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#14100E`. The deepest ground level for canvas backgrounds.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** `#29201B` bordered with 1px `#3D3028`. Subtle, warm ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(10, 8, 7, 0.6)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Sheets & Menus):** `#362B25` bordered with 1px `#4A3C33`. `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.
- **Ember Glows:** Interactive focal points (hero primary buttons, floating timers) utilize an ambient color-tinted aura: `0 4px 24px 0 rgba(255, 122, 41, 0.28)`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature uses plush, rounded contours to complement the friendly warmth of the system.

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Stepper buttons, tooltips, and badges.
- **Card & Container Radius (1rem / 16px):** Recipe browsing cards, search inputs, modal dialogs, and bottom sheets.
- **Extended Radius (1.5rem / 24px):** Large hero containers and dietary highlights.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Cuisine category chips, primary action buttons, search pill boundaries, and segmented control thumbs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Full pill shape, filled with `#FF7A29`, label in dark espresso `#14100E` (`label-lg`), height 52px. Accompanied by a subtle orange ambient glow.
- **Secondary Action:** Pill shape, background `#362B25`, border 1px `#3D3028`, label `#FBF3EB`.
- **Tonal Green Accent:** Used for dietary actions or cook completion; filled with `#48A560`, label `#FBF3EB`.

### Cuisine Chips
- **Resting:** Pill container, height 36px, background `#29201B`, border 1px `#3D3028`, text `#B3A296`.
- **Selected:** Pill container, filled with `#FF7A29`, text `#14100E`, with bold font weight.
- **Icon Support:** Includes 16px cuisine glyphs or flags with 6px right spacing to the label.

### Recipe Cards
- **Structure:** 16px border-radius (`rounded-lg`), background `#29201B`, border 1px `#3D3028`.
- **Media:** 16:9 or 4:3 rounded top imagery, smooth inner bottom gradient mask overlaying metadata.
- **Content:** Title in `headline-sm`, cooking time and difficulty level in `body-sm` using text secondary `#B3A296`. Favorite bookmark icon docked in the top-right over a translucent circular scrim (`rgba(20, 16, 14, 0.6)`).

### Input Fields & Dark Search Bar
- **Search Bar:** Pill-shaped, height 52px, surface fill `#1E1714`, outline 1px `#3D3028`. Left-docked magnifying lens in `#B3A296`, placeholder text in `#B3A296`. Focus state transitions outline to `#FF7A29` with a gentle 2px outer glow.

### Segmented Controls
- **Track:** Height 44px, background `#1E1714`, inner padding 4px, border-radius 12px.
- **Thumb:** Background `#362B25`, border 1px `#3D3028`, pill/rounded-md geometry, text `#FBF3EB` (`label-md`).

### Checkboxes & Step Counters
- **Checkboxes (Ingredients):** 22px rounded square (6px radius), border 2px `#3D3028`. Checked state fills `#48A560` with a cream `#FBF3EB` check mark, striking through ingredient text in `#B3A296`.
- **Steppers (Servings / Timers):** Segmented pill enclosing minus button, numeric count (`title-md` in `#FBF3EB`), and plus button on surface container high `#362B25`.
