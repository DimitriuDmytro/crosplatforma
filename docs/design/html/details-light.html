Chef's Golden Crust Tip

Pat salmon completely dry with paper towels before seasoning for that ultimate restaurant-grade sear!

Tap to mark completed
STEP 1 schedule 3 min

Season the salmon fillets on both sides with salt and pepper. Heat olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat.

STEP 2 schedule 10 min

Sear salmon for 5 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through. Transfer fillets carefully to a warm plate and set aside.

STEP 3 schedule 3 min

In the same skillet, melt remaining butter and sauté minced garlic for 1 minute until fragrant. Add halved cherry tomatoes and blister lightly.

STEP 4 schedule 5 min

Pour in heavy cream and bring to a gentle simmer. Stir in baby spinach and grated parmesan until spinach wilts and sauce becomes silky and thick.

STEP 5 schedule 4 min

Return salmon to the skillet, spoon creamy sauce over fillets, and garnish generously with fresh basil leaves. Serve immediately warm!
