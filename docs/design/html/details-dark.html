Creamy Tuscan Garlic Butter Salmon

1 Prep & Season Salmon
3 min

Season the salmon fillets on both sides with salt and pepper. Heat olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a large heavy-bottomed skillet over medium-high heat.

2 Pan-Sear to Golden Crisp
timer 5m / side

Sear salmon for 5 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through. Transfer to a clean plate and keep lightly covered.

3 Sauté Aromatics & Tomatoes
2 min

In the same skillet, melt remaining butter and sauté minced garlic for 1 minute until fragrant. Add halved cherry tomatoes and blister slightly.

4 Simmer Tuscan Cream Sauce
4 min

Pour in heavy cream and bring to a gentle simmer. Stir in baby spinach and parmesan cheese until the spinach wilts and the sauce thickens into velvet.

5 Assemble & Garnish
Finish

Return salmon to the skillet, spoon creamy sauce generously over the fillets, and garnish with fresh basil. Serve immediately warm with crusty artisan bread or pasta!
